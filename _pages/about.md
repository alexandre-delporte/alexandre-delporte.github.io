I am a postdoctoral researcher at the [Laboratoire d'Écologie Alpine (LECA)](https://leca.osug.fr/) in Chambéry, France, working with Vincent Miele and Anne Loison. I work on short-term interactions between species, using camera trap data and point process models.

I did my PhD in statistics at the [Laboratoire Jean Kuntzmann](https://www-ljk.imag.fr/) (Grenoble, France), supervised by [Adeline Samson](http://adeline.e-samson.org/), in collaboration with [Susanne Ditlevsen](https://web.math.ku.dk/~susanne/). During my PhD, I developed probabilistic models and inference methods for complex, noisy, and constrained movement data, with a focus on state-space models, mixed-effects models, and stochastic differential equations applied to marine mammal movement ecology.

See my [Publications](/publications/) and [CV](/cv/) for more details.
