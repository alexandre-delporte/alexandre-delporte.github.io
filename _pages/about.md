I am a PhD student in statistics at the [Laboratoire Jean Kuntzmann](https://www-ljk.imag.fr/) (Grenoble, France), supervised by [Adeline Samson](http://adeline.e-samson.org/). I am a member of the [SVH](https://svh.imag.fr/) team and collaborate with [Susanne Ditlevsen](https://web.math.ku.dk/~susanne/).

I work in statistics applied to movement ecology. I develop probabilistic models and inference methods for complex, noisy, and constrained movement data, with a focus on state-space models, mixed-effects models, and stochastic differential equations applied to marine mammal movement ecology.

See my [Publications](/publications/) and [CV](/cv/) for more details.
